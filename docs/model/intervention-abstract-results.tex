The revised computational study compares five fixed policies on 100 fresh audiences each. Original staff instructions yield a mean of 195.2 s; coordinated staff alone yield 175.9 s. The preselected dynamic-LED and partial-belt combination yields 172.5 s, with 77/100 replications clearing by 180 s. These are fixed-model results, not Bailey measurements. Slower response and material time-step sensitivity limit their operational interpretation.
